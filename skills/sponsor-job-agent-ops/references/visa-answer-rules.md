# Visa answer rules

Answer current-authorisation questions from the candidate's current approved route state, not from possible future eligibility.

Example: a candidate who appears eligible for Hong Kong TTPS but has not received/activated it is not yet currently authorised to work in Hong Kong. Employer-sponsorship requirements and current work authorisation are separate questions.

For UK/NL/DE/IE/HK, show route, evidence, unresolved checks, and confidence where the system records them. Do not replace official-registry/ruleset evidence with generic web assumptions.
